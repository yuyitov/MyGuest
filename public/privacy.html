Privacy & Data

Last updated: July 2026

Short version: We collect the information you provide to create your guest guide. Private details (WiFi, access codes, host phone) are stored in a secure system separate from the public website and are never published publicly. We do not sell your data.

What we collect

When you use MyGuest, we collect the following information:

  • Property name and description
  • Host name and contact email
  • Property location and address
  • Check-in / check-out times and instructions
  • House rules and amenities
  • Local recommendations
  • WiFi credentials (SSID and password)
  • Access codes (door code, building code, lockbox code)
  • Private host phone number
  • Any files or materials you upload (PDF, images, documents)
  • Payment information processed by Stripe (we do not store card data)

How we use your data

All information collected is used solely to create, deliver and maintain your welcome book guide. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any purpose beyond guide production and support.

Public vs. private data separation

This separation is a core part of how MyGuest protects your guests' experience without exposing sensitive information.

  • Property name, rules, amenities, recommendations — public guide (myguestguide.com), reachable by anyone with the guide link.
  • WiFi credentials, access codes, host phone, private access details — secure encrypted system (Cloudflare), never on the public website, reachable only through the private token-protected link shared with the host.
  • Host email, payment info — Cloudflare secure system / Stripe. MyGuest only, never shared publicly.

Third-party processors

MyGuest uses the following services to operate. Each is bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Tally — information form collection
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Cloudflare — hosting, secure data processing, and private data storage
  • GitHub Pages — public guide hosting

We do not sell your data to any third party.

Data retention

We retain your guide data (public and private) for up to 12 months after delivery. After that period, data may be deleted as part of routine cleanup. You may request earlier deletion at any time.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, email hello@myguestguide.com with your property name and the email address used at checkout. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about your data? Email us at hello@myguestguide.com. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday.
