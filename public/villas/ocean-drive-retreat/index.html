Getting There

Open Google Maps
Address
800 Ocean Drive, Miami Beach, FL 33139
Directions
Located directly on Ocean Drive in the heart of the Art Deco Historic District. From Miami International Airport, take SR-836 E to I-195 E, then head south on Alton Rd to Ocean Drive — approximately 30 minutes.
Transport
Rideshare: Uber and Lyft are widely available throughout South Beach. Citi Bike stations are within a 2-minute walk. The South Beach Local circulator bus runs nearby and is free of charge.

Things to Know

Good to Know

Before You Leave
Strip used bedding and leave towels in the bathtub.
Dispose of food waste before departing.
