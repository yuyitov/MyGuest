This is basic operational and commercial policy text written for clarity. It is not legal advice. If you need legal counsel, please consult a qualified attorney.

1. What you receive

MyGuest creates a digital welcome book for your vacation rental. Your guide includes:

  • A mobile-friendly guest guide accessible via secure link
  • A printable PDF version for hosts
  • Content in English, Spanish and French

Delivery is via secure email link sent to the address used at checkout.

2. Delivery timeline

We aim to deliver your completed guide within 3–5 business days after you submit the completed information form. This is a manually reviewed service — delivery is not instant. Timelines may vary if submitted materials need clarification or if you upload files that require manual extraction.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for providing accurate and complete information about your property. MyGuest formats and presents the information you provide. We are not responsible for errors, omissions, or inaccuracies in information submitted by the host.

If incorrect information is provided and a correction is needed, the included correction rounds cover two revision cycles.

4. Corrections

Two free correction rounds are included with every guide. You may request corrections using the link provided in your delivery email. That link stays valid until you have used both rounds.

Additional correction rounds cost $6 USD / $59 MXN each. Requests for significant content changes that go beyond a correction round — such as major content rewrites or full redesigns — may require an extra fee, which we will communicate before proceeding.

Corrections are reviewed and applied manually. They are not instant.

5. Uploaded files and materials

If you upload files (PDF, Word, Canva exports, photos, or screenshots), we use them to extract information for your guide. Uploaded files may be stored temporarily during the production process. We do not share your files with third parties.

If files require manual review, delivery may take additional time. You will be notified if this applies.

6. Private information

WiFi passwords, access codes, door codes, and private host contact information are stored securely in an encrypted system separate from the public website. They are delivered only through a private, token-protected link and are never published on the public page of your guide.

See our Privacy & Data page for full details.

7. Data deletion

To request deletion of your data (guide content and private details), email hello@myguestguide.com with your property name and the email address used at checkout. Requests are processed within 30 days.

8. Refunds

You get a full refund if you ask for it before submitting the information form, and also within 7 calendar days of delivery — in that case we take your guide offline immediately. Full details and the remaining scenarios are in our Refund Policy.

9. Invoicing

Your Stripe receipt is issued automatically. If you need a Mexican tax invoice (CFDI), request it with your tax details at hello@myguestguide.com within the same calendar month as your purchase — after that window, Mexican tax rules no longer allow it to be issued.

10. Support

For questions or issues, contact us at hello@myguestguide.com. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday.

12. Governing language
